How to play

Find the hidden word before your tries run out. Every guess must be a real word, and every guess tells you something.

Three difficulties

  • Easy — a five-letter word in six tries.
  • Medium — seven letters in seven tries.
  • Hard — nine letters in eight tries.

Each difficulty keeps its own daily puzzle, its own archive and its own statistics.

Beyond today

  • The archive. Every puzzle back to No. 1 is still playable.
  • Challenges. Pick a word and send someone a link to it.
  • Hints. If a round starts going badly, a nudge is offered under the board. Taking it is up to you.
  • The read-out. When a round ends, see how much ground each guess actually covered.

A new puzzle arrives at midnight, your local time.
