A tiny 2D sandbox. Dig, build, survive the night.

Controls

Left Click Mine / Attack   Right Click / F Place / Eat

1-9 Hotbar   E Inventory & Crafting   Esc Pause

On touch: tap-hold to mine, quick tap to place.

Eat food (right click) to stay fed; hold your breath underwater or you'll start drowning.

Mine obsidian with a diamond pickaxe, build a doorway of it, then light it with Flint and Steel to open a Nether portal.
