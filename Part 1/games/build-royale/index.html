BUILD ROYALE

A 2D side-view battle royale. Build walls, ramps and floors for cover — everyone else is armed and shooting.

Bots

How to Play

Move: A/D or ←/→   Jump: W / Space   Aim & Shoot: Mouse + Left Click

Build: 1 Wall   2 Floor   3 Ramp   4 Pistol   5 Picked-up weapon   — building costs wood, structures can be shot down.

You start with only a sidearm pistol — find Rifle, SMG, Shotgun and Sniper crates on the ground for a stronger weapon with limited ammo. Bots hunt for them too.

Watch the storm — the safe zone shrinks over time and damages anyone outside it. Last one standing wins.
