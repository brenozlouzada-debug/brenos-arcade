Punch and Kick are fast. Heavy Punch and Heavy Kick are slower but hit much harder (Heavy Kick even pops the foe into the air). Low Kick is a quick poke. Grab can't be blocked, so use it to punish an opponent who's turtling. Hold Down/Crouch while blocking to reduce damage. Land hits to fill your POWER meter, then unleash your character's unique Special!
