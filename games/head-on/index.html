How to Play

Move: WASD / Arrow Keys  •  Pause: Esc

Hitting the opponent's side or back with your own front wrecks you — it's like slamming a wall. To score, make them hit your side or back with their front instead. Two fronts colliding head-on wrecks both — nobody scores. A front-first wall hit also wrecks you; reversing into a wall is safe.
