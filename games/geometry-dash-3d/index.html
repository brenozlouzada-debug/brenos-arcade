Guide

Controls

Gamemodes

Pads touch one and it fires automatically

Orbs tap while inside one to fire it — they don't trigger on their own

Portals

Mode portals switch how you move. Speed portals change your scroll speed — higher speeds cover more ground per second but shrink your reaction window.

Difficulty

Building your own level

  • The editor's grid is exactly this legend, laid out on a timeline — pick a piece, click a lane cell to place it.
  • Portals span all three lanes, so they get their own row at the top instead of a lane.
  • Orbs and coins share a cell with each other but not with ground pieces — a spike and an orb can sit on the same cell.
  • There's no auto-checker for whether a layout is beatable — use Play Test often while you build.
